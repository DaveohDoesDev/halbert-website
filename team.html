About Us

Local people. Real answers.

Halbert Insurance Services is an independent agency based in Melbourne, Florida. We help individuals, families, and businesses find quality, affordable coverage, and we stay with them long after they enroll.

Our Story

Built on responsiveness.

Brandon Halbert founded the agency on a simple idea: insurance is confusing, so people deserve a broker who answers the phone, replies to texts, and explains things clearly.

Today our team covers everything from Medicare and ACA plans to small business group benefits and life insurance. We're licensed, independent, and on your side.

What we stand for

  • Clarity: plain-English answers, no jargon
  • Choice: 12+ carriers, not just one
  • Care: fast replies and year-round support
